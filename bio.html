Professional Bio

I lead advanced analytics and AI for pharma commercial teams, covering targeting, forecasting, and promotional effectiveness, and increasingly agentic AI that automates recurring analytical work. I am currently Director of Advanced Analytics within Go-to-Market Capabilities (GTMC) at Sanofi. Previously, I worked at Merck within the Digital Human Health organization and at Eli Lilly in the Advanced Analytics & Data Science (AADS) organization.

My training is in epidemiology: I hold a Ph.D. in Public Health (600+ citations) and an M.S. in Data Science from Indiana University Bloomington. That training shapes how I think about AI. Output is cheap; output you can act on isn't. I focus on the review gates, traceability, and human accountability that make AI-built analysis trustworthy.

I also build. Through Overdue Studio, my independent product studio, I ship small AI-built apps on my own time. It keeps my judgment about what agents can and can't do grounded in practice, not slides. I write about what I learn on my blog.
