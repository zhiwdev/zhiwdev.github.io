Professional Bio

I am an advanced analytics and AI leader in the pharmaceutical industry. I am currently a Director of Advanced Analytics at Sanofi, where I lead analytics and AI for commercial teams, covering targeting, forecasting, and promotional effectiveness, and increasingly agentic AI that automates recurring analytical work.

Before Sanofi, I spent four years at Merck. As Associate Director of Data Science for Vaccines and Infectious Diseases, I led targeting and segmentation, marketing mix, forecasting, and health policy analytics, and deployed agentic AI to automate segmentation and targeting workflows. Earlier, in Digital Solutions, I grew a team of data scientists and engineers from 2 to 18 and delivered a cloud-based forecasting platform that reached 95% adoption across three brands. Before Merck, I was a Senior Data Scientist in payer analytics at Eli Lilly, and I worked in real-world data research at Elevance Health and pharmacy analytics at CVS Health.

My training is in epidemiology: I hold a Ph.D. in Public Health (600+ citations) and an M.S. in Data Science from Indiana University Bloomington, and an MPA in Survey Methods from the University of Illinois Chicago. That training shapes how I think about AI. Output is cheap; output you can act on isn't. I care about analytics that are practical, scalable, and decision-focused, and about the review gates, traceability, and human accountability that make AI-built analysis trustworthy.

I also build. Whether through internal platforms or, on my own time, through Overdue Studio, my independent product studio, I like turning ideas into real products. Shipping small AI-built apps keeps my judgment about what agents can and can't do grounded in practice, not slides. I write about what I learn on my blog.
