Professional Bio

I am a data science and analytics professional with a focus on applied analytics, leadership, and product-oriented problem solving in the pharmaceutical industry. I currently work at Sanofi. Previously, I worked at Merck within the Digital Human Health organization and at Eli Lilly in the Advanced Analytics & Data Science (AADS) organization.

My interests center on building analytics that are practical, scalable, and decision-focused. I am particularly drawn to analytics leadership, applied AI, and turning ideas into real products—whether through internal platforms, experimental projects, or side-hustle applications.

I hold a Ph.D. in Public Health and an M.S. in Data Science from Indiana University Bloomington. Outside of work, I build AI-powered apps and lightweight tools focused on real life problems.
