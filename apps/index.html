I build focused products designed to remove everyday friction.

Overdue Studio

Overdue Studio is my independent product studio for building lightweight, beautifully designed tools that help people handle the things they keep putting off, because they are awkward, annoying, or simply too high-friction.

I am developing a focused portfolio across three collections: Overdue Wellness (private, judgment-free health tools like NotEmbarrassing), Overdue Life (everyday coordination tools like birthday party invites with accurate headcounts), and Overdue Dev (fast utilities like an icon crop tool that removes workflow pain).

My strategy is to ship narrow, high-signal products with premium UX, privacy-first defaults, and delight features that matter, then iterate quickly based on real user feedback. Each app stands on its own, but together they create a consistent Overdue Studio experience: calm, practical, and crafted for everyday life.
